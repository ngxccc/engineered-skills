# Writing Polish Eval

Use this checklist after editing a draft. Every check must pass before returning the final text to the user.

For **Detect** requests, ensure the output lists each flagged pattern with:

1. The quoted line from the draft.
2. The specific pattern name from the taxonomy.
3. A concise, actionable fix.
   Do not rewrite the piece, score it, or guess AI authorship during detection.

---

## 1. Core voice & intention

- [ ] **Preserves authorial voice:** Does the text keep the writer's characteristic vocabulary, cadence, humor, bluntness, and tolerance for informality?
- [ ] **No invented facts or opinions:** Are all claims, examples, and arguments strictly grounded in the user's draft, with no hallucinatory additions?
- [ ] **Proportional editing:** Did the edit touch only the slop and structural bottlenecks, leaving strong, expressive human sentences alone?
- [ ] **Intact spoken rhythm:** Does the text retain natural sentence variations, colloquial phrasing, or fragments where they feel authentic to the writer?

## 2. Lexical & phrase checks

- [ ] **Zero banned buzzwords:** Are words like _delve, foster, leverage, utilize, robust, streamline, cutting-edge, tapestry, realm, beacon_ eliminated?
- [ ] **No empty adverbs:** Are qualifiers like _crucially, fundamentally, inherently, truly, actually_ removed unless they carry genuine weight?
- [ ] **No throat-clearing openers:** Are introductory filler phrases (_"It's worth noting"_, _"When it comes to"_, _"At the end of the day"_) stripped away?

## 3. Structural & syntactic habits

- [ ] **No trailing `-ing` preachiness:** Are dangling participle clauses (_highlighting_, _underscoring_, _reflecting_) replaced with direct actions or deleted?
- [ ] **No automatic tricolons:** Are lists of three adjectives or nouns (_"innovative, scalable, and robust"_) eliminated in favor of precise single terms or concrete descriptions?
- [ ] **Direct claims over binary contrasts:** Are constructions like _"This is not X. It's Y."_ replaced with direct statements of Y?
- [ ] **No faux-insight colon reveals:** Are dramatic hooks (_"The catch: ..."_, _"Here's what nobody tells you: ..."_) rewritten as natural prose?
- [ ] **No canned ending tropes:** Does the piece end on an actual point or next action, rather than an aphoristic kicker or a repetitive _"In conclusion"_ recap?

## 4. Grounding & attribution

- [ ] **Passes the Portability Test:** Does every major sentence apply specifically to this subject, without interchangeable generic fluff?
- [ ] **Fact-grounded over puffery:** Are claims of historical significance (_"marking a pivotal moment"_) scaled back to plain, verifiable facts?
- [ ] **Concrete attribution:** Are vague authorities (_"experts agree"_, _"studies show"_) either named specifically or removed?
- [ ] **No authorial hand-holding:** Is interpretive metadiscourse (_"The key takeaway here is"_, _"As you can see"_) cut?

## 5. Cadence & formatting

- [ ] **Varied sentence and paragraph rhythm:** Does the draft avoid robotic symmetry where every paragraph is roughly equal in length and tone?
- [ ] **Clean formatting:** Are random mid-sentence bolds, excessive bulleted lists, and heading emojis removed so the prose carries itself?
- [ ] **Restrained punctuation:** Are em dashes used sparingly (zero in short copy, at most 1–2 in longer essays)?

## 6. Delivery quality

- [ ] **Natural read-aloud test:** Would this sound like an intelligent, articulate human colleague reading their own draft out loud?
- [ ] **Transparency:** Does the response include both the complete edited draft and a brief **What changed** breakdown explaining the key edits?
